PECARN Pediatric Head Injury

Age-specific minor blunt head trauma decision-rule reference · Ages <18 years · GCS 14–15

Clinical decision support, not diagnosis. Use only after a qualified clinician has assessed the patient and verified applicability. A very-low-risk category does not exclude concussion, other injuries, or the need for reassessment. If examination is concerning, seek urgent clinical evaluation.

Assessment

No individual ciTBI probability is calculated. Clinical decision-making may differ based on deterioration, multiple findings, infant age <3 months, and clinical circumstances.

Scope and interpretation

  1. High-risk branch: The original algorithm recommends CT.
  2. Intermediate-risk branch: Observation versus CT; consider additional clinical factors and family preference.
  3. Very-low-risk branch: CT generally not indicated for most otherwise eligible children.

Privacy: All assessment logic runs in this browser; there is no backend call, analytics, or local data storage. Do not enter patient identifiers.

Primary reference: Kuppermann et al., Lancet (2009), Figure 3.

Guidance: CDC pediatric mTBI recommendations.

Educational reference implementation. Not independently clinically validated, regulated as a medical device, or intended for autonomous CT decisions.
